# Compliance, Trust & Affiliate Disclosure

This document is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Confirm current rules on each platform before acting.

## Affiliate transparency
- Clearly disclose when content contains affiliate links or may earn commission.
- Use platform-provided disclosure features where available and required.
- Keep disclosure understandable, visible, and close to the recommendation/link.
- Do not imply independent testing if the product was not personally tested.

## Product claims
- Only make claims supported by reliable evidence or actual experience.
- State limitations, conditions, and who the product may not suit.
- Avoid false urgency, fake scarcity, fabricated reviews, and guaranteed outcomes.
- Use extra caution for beauty, health, wellness, safety, and regulated categories.

## Content assets and intellectual property
- Use original content, properly licensed assets, or seller/platform assets that are explicitly permitted for affiliate use.
- Do not assume that a publicly visible video or photo is free to reuse.
- Keep a record of asset source and permitted use.

## Data and platform access
- Use public, authorized, or explicitly permitted data sources.
- Do not bypass login gates, rate limits, or platform restrictions.
- Do not collect private messages or unnecessary personal information.
- Review official program terms, eligibility, link rules, and prohibited product categories.

## Product trust
- Check seller identity, return/refund terms, listing consistency, and authenticity signals.
- Recheck volatile price and commission terms before publishing.
- Do not promise delivery, stock, or current pricing without verification.
