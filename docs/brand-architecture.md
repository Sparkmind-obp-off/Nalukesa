# Brand Architecture

## Vision
Become a trusted, evidence-led affiliate discovery umbrella that learns across niches and helps people discover useful products without confusing popularity with proof.

## Mission
1. Systematically research niches, audiences, offers, and product-level demand signals.
2. Run low-cost, ethical content experiments.
3. Turn real outcomes into decisions about what to test, improve, hold, or scale.
4. Publish useful curated discoveries only when evidence and disclosure standards are met.

## Positioning
NALUKESA is an **affiliate discovery and intelligence hub**. It is the parent brand and learning system—not a single-niche creator identity and not a marketplace at launch.

Working brand line: **Discover better. Choose smarter.** This is a proposal, not a legally cleared or registered slogan.

## Architecture
- **NALUKESA (umbrella):** operating principles, central research, portfolio view, shared design language.
- **Niche identities:** audience-specific content channels when platform eligibility and capacity allow.
- **Experiments:** bounded hypotheses attached to a niche, offer, creative angle, and time window.
- **Product intelligence:** source-linked product records, offer economics, risks, and observed outcomes.
- **Public discovery layer (optional later):** selected product guides or curated links with clear affiliate disclosures.

Example working channel names—not registration guarantees:
- NALUKESA Beauty
- NALUKESA Wear
- NALUKESA Home

Use platform-native account names only after checking availability, eligibility, and naming rules. Keep each channel's promise specific; do not publish duplicate low-value content across channels.

## Vision vs evidence
The initial lanes—beauty & personal care, everyday fashion, and useful home goods—are hypotheses, not confirmed winners. The system must allow a niche to be rejected or replaced.

## Brand voice
- Calm, discerning, useful, honest.
- Specific instead of sensational.
- Helpful, not pushy.
- Premium through restraint and clarity, not excessive decoration.
- No guaranteed results, false urgency, fake scarcity, or unsupported performance claims.

## Visual identity
- Base: near-black / charcoal.
- Surface: deep graphite with subtle warm undertones.
- Accent: muted champagne gold, used sparingly.
- Secondary: restrained sage for positive/verified states.
- Typography: modern system sans-serif; spacious hierarchy and readable body text.
- Motifs: editorial grids, thin rules, precise labels, understated circular geometry.
- Avoid: loud gradients, excessive glass effects, neon accents, cluttered cards, fake live metrics.

Prototype palette: `#10110F`, `#171916`, `#C9AA78`, `#A8B69A`, `#F4F0E7`. Treat these as design tokens subject to user approval.

## Naming and legal caution
A domain or initial search that appears clear is not the same as legal clearance. Before material investment, verify current Indonesian trademark records and relevant classes with qualified support. Do not claim trademark registration unless confirmed.
