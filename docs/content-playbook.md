# Content Playbook

## Principles
Useful before persuasive. Demonstrate before claiming. Be specific about limitations. Disclose affiliate relationships clearly in the content and/or platform-required placement. Follow current platform and local legal requirements.

## Repeatable formats
1. **Problem → demonstration:** state a real, common problem; show how the product functions; identify who it may not suit.
2. **Feature in context:** demonstrate one verifiable feature instead of listing vague superlatives.
3. **Honest first impression:** describe what was actually tested; distinguish initial impression from long-term durability.
4. **FAQ / objection:** answer a real audience question with a source or clearly state what remains unknown.
5. **Comparison:** explain the comparison criteria, relevant differences, and what evidence is missing.
6. **Routine / use case:** show how and when an item is used, without implying universal results.
7. **Value framing:** explain price and trade-offs based on current information; don't claim “cheapest” without a current, defined comparison.

## Hook examples (adapt to evidence)
- “Sebelum pilih [kategori], cek tiga hal ini.”
- “Yang perlu kamu tahu sebelum membeli [produk].”
- “Aku membandingkan [fitur A] dan [fitur B]—ini bedanya.”
- “Produk ini mungkin cocok kalau kebutuhanmu [situasi spesifik].”

These are structures, not claims about a particular product.

## Creative testing
Change one major variable at a time where practical: hook, angle, demonstration, audience framing, or CTA. Keep a record of what changed. Avoid uploading near-identical repetitive content solely to flood feeds.

## Claims and rights
- Do not invent product experience, testimonials, endorsements, sales volume, scarcity, or results.
- Do not use seller/creator video without permission or a platform-authorized affiliate asset mechanism.
- Use platform-provided product links and permitted assets according to their terms.
- Beauty, wellness, and safety-related claims need particular caution.
- Never fabricate before/after images or misrepresent editing.
- Track whether a product was personally tested, researched only, or demonstrated by an authorized source.
