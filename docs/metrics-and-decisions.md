# Metrics & Decision Rules

## Funnel
1. Output: pieces published, production minutes, test coverage.
2. Attention: impressions/views and retention metrics where available.
3. Intent: profile visits, product clicks, saves, qualified questions where available.
4. Conversion: attributed orders and order conversion where available.
5. Quality: approved commission, cancellations/returns, order approval rate, product complaints.
6. Efficiency: approved commission per production hour; only calculate when underlying data is available and comparable.

## Non-negotiable distinctions
- Views are not clicks.
- Clicks are not orders.
- Orders are not approved commissions.
- Advertised commission is not earned commission.
- No data is not zero.
- A single viral post is not automatically a repeatable system.

## Pre-register a decision rule
Before publishing, define:
- Time window and minimum content scope.
- Primary metric available in the platform.
- Minimum evidence required to continue.
- Conditions for iterate, hold, stop, or scale.
- Maximum time or spend budget.

Example framework—not a guaranteed benchmark:
- **Scale candidate:** repeatable qualified intent and/or approved earnings across more than one piece, with acceptable cancellation/return quality and manageable production effort.
- **Iterate:** some promising intent, but creative or offer uncertainty remains.
- **Hold / inconclusive:** too little reach, data unavailable, attribution lag, or test scope not completed.
- **Stop:** repeated lack of meaningful intent after a fair test, poor product trust, policy risk, or unworkable economics.

Do not invent universal numerical thresholds. Set thresholds based on platform baselines, current evidence, and the purpose of each experiment.

## Weekly review
1. What did we actually observe?
2. Which source supports each claim?
3. What changed compared with the previous period?
4. Which results are attributable versus ambiguous?
5. What was the time cost?
6. What is still unknown?
7. Decision and reason?
8. Next test, owner, and review date?

## Portfolio decision
A niche should earn additional accounts, content capacity, paid tools, or spend through repeatable evidence. Keep the number of simultaneous experiments small enough to measure and complete.
